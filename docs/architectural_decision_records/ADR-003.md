# ADR-003 - PANORAMA External Validation: Pipeline, Geometry, and Compute
**Date:** 2026-09-17
**Status:** Accepted
**Author:** Leander Antony A

---

## Context

The Q1 plan requires external multi-centre CT validation to break the thesis's dataset-of-origin
confound. As of 2026-09 the PANORAMA dataset (2,238 CT studies, the largest public PDAC cohort) is
downloaded, verified, and audited. Building the CT pipeline on it forced several decisions, some
against the field's defaults. Full technical detail lives in the local `docs/preprocessing_audit.md`
section 5 (gitignored); this ADR records the decisions themselves in the tracked history.

Audited facts driving the decisions:

- No institution/centre column in the metadata -> per-site class balance is unverifiable.
- Scanner/manufacturer is a measured confound (Cramer's V = 0.44); the `level` field is near-label
  leakage (it encodes diagnostic-confirmation method).
- The provided masks are label-correlated (479 of 482 manual masks are PDAC), so cropping from them
  would leak the label.
- Local hardware is an 8 GB RTX 5060 Ti (Blackwell); full 3D nnU-Net training needs more.

## Decision

1. **Go Q1-direct; the Q2 reframe becomes a fallback.** The data blocker is gone, and the two
   papers share too much narrative for Q2-first to be safe.
2. **The CT contribution is a comparative evaluation of debiasing methods, not a proposed method.**
   Because the DomainBed literature shows a well-tuned ERM matches the domain-generalization family,
   the panel is tuned ERM / DFR / gradient reversal / SSL-foundation backbone under one
   pre-registered model-selection rule. A rigorous negative result is the contribution.
3. **Input geometry is 2.5D** for the mitigation study (swappable 2D backbone for DFR and 2D
   foundation models; 8 GB VRAM), with a **3D nnU-Net detection reference** to anchor performance
   against the field.
4. **ROI localization crops to PANORAMA's provided pancreas (4) + duct (5) segmentation, never the
   lesion mask (label 1).** Fixed **150x100x40 mm** margin (validated: 100% lesion + pancreas
   containment). Only the *lesion* mask leaks the label (manual/auto provenance correlates with
   PDAC); the pancreas/duct segmentations are auto-generated uniformly for every case and are
   label-blind. This revises an earlier draft that used our own segmenter: the field trains its own
   only to deploy without masks, which is not our constraint, and TotalSegmentator empirically
   failed on ~5-14% of scans while the provided mask covers all cases.
5. **Evaluation headline is leave-one-manufacturer-out** (patient-grouped), with a 5-fold stratified
   in-distribution reference and MSD held out as a true external source; NIH (all-control) is used
   only for specificity.
6. **Hybrid compute:** the 3D reference runs on a rented GPU chosen **reliability-first** (RTX A6000
   48 GB or RTX 4090 24 GB > RTX PRO 4500 32 GB > RTX 5090, which has a virtualization reset bug and
   is risky for unattended rental). Network volumes require Secure Cloud. Reduced scope (1 fold or
   250 epochs) is a sufficient reference at ~$15-25; full 5-fold x1000ep is ~$80-100. Everything else
   runs locally on 8 GB. The ~53 GB cropped dataset lives on a region-flexible **Global volume**,
   staged to the pod's local disk per run (never trained off network storage); the 182 GB raw set is
   never uploaded. H100-class GPUs are not worth it (cost scales worse than speed; nnU-Net is often
   dataloader-bound).

## Consequences

**Positive:** matches field preprocessing where it matters (segment -> crop -> classify,
HU-preserving nnU-Net normalization), keeps the honest bias-aware framing, and is tractable on
commodity hardware.

**Negative / risks:** 2.5D deviates from the unanimously-3D field (mitigated by the 3D reference and
an ablation); the scanner-confound novelty is an *assembly* of known parts (differentiate via two
independent confounds + the `level` leakage finding); PANORAMA's "class decoupled from source"
cannot be locally verified and must be stated as a limitation.

## Future Work

- Complete the ROI crop, then the 3D reference and the 2.5D mitigation panel (Milestone A go/no-go:
  does the scanner confound propagate into learned features, and does any mitigation remove it?).
- Benchmark against ShortKit-ML.
- Defer the paired CT+biomarker fusion cohort to paper #2.
