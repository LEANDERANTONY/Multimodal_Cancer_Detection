# ADR-004 - After Milestone A: 3D Model, Tight ROI, Evaluation Trio, Bounded-Exploitation Framing
**Date:** 2026-10-05
**Status:** Accepted (partially supersedes ADR-003, decisions 3–6)
**Author:** Leander Antony A

---

## Context

ADR-003 planned a 2.5D mitigation study anchored by a one-off 3D nnU-Net reference, cropped with a
150×100×40 mm margin, evaluated headline-first by leave-one-manufacturer-out (LOMO). Between
2026-09-28 and 2026-10-05 the 3D reference was trained and analysed in full (loose and tight crops,
5-fold CV and LOMO, detection scoring, feature probe, confound tax, external validation). Milestone A
reported, and several of ADR-003's assumptions changed. Results: `docs/modeling_pipeline.md`.

Facts that drove the decisions:

- **Milestone A outcome:** no LOMO generalization gap, and scanner is only weakly decodable from the
  encoder (0.59–0.64 vs 0.50 shuffled). By ADR-003's own rule this is the "confound does not reach the
  images (much)" branch.
- **The tight crop (100×50×15 mm, the PANORAMA baseline/PanDx margin) is clearly better than the loose
  crop:** CV lesion Dice 0.33 → 0.505, detection AUROC 0.70 → 0.79.
- **Confound tax with a CI:** tight +0.031 [+0.010, +0.053] — small but not zero.
- **External MSD (new hospital, both classes):** tight AUROC 0.82 [0.76, 0.88], Dice 0.555.
- **Tight-LOMO fold 2 collapsed** to all-background on its first run (per-sample Dice rewards empty
  predictions; fold 2 has the lowest positive rate, ~21%); a re-run with the same configuration trained
  normally.

## Decision

1. **The 3D nnU-Net is the main model of the paper, not just a reference.** The mitigation panel stays
   2.5D and local (ADR-005), and is compared against the 3D results.
2. **Tight ROI (100×50×15 mm, from the provided pancreas + duct masks) is the primary crop**; the loose
   crop is kept as an ablation. The crop source is unchanged from ADR-003 (label-blind masks, never the
   lesion mask).
3. **Evaluation is a trio, applied to every model:** 5-fold CV (incl. the confound tax with a
   patient-level bootstrap CI), LOMO (Siemens / Toshiba / Philips held out; GE n=46 too small), and the
   held-out external sets (MSD for AUROC/Dice, NIH for specificity — **never pooled**, since a pooled
   MSD+NIH AUROC is confounded by dataset of origin).
4. **Framing: "the confound is present in the data but only weakly exploited (bounded at ≈0.05 AUROC),
   with no penalty on new manufacturers or a new hospital."** Not "not exploited" (the tight CI excludes
   zero), and not "exploited" (no LOMO or external penalty). The loose model's tax CI is being computed.
5. **The paper stays Q1 by adding the deployment-ROI experiment** (ADR-005) as the main novelty, plus the
   mitigation panel, rather than pivoting to a smaller venue.
6. **Operational:** paper-grade runs on rented GPUs chosen by vCPU + RAM + price (nnU-Net is
   dataloader-bound, ~5.5 GB VRAM); every completed run is mirrored locally (`models/nnunet/`,
   `docs/data_layout.md`); per-case scores are saved so no metric needs re-inference.

## Consequences

**Positive:** a field-matched model with strong external performance; a sharper, quantified claim; all
evidence (summaries, per-case CSVs, logs) tracked in `reports/nnunet_summaries/`.

**Negative / risks:** the "confound exploited" story the project set out to show did not materialise on
PANORAMA, so novelty rests on the bounded-exploitation measurement, the deployment-ROI experiment and the
mitigation panel; the fold-2 collapse must be reported in the methods.
